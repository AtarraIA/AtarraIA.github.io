# ── Sobre nosotros ────────────────────────────────────────────────

[titulo.sobre]
about *u*s

[texto.sobre]
AtarraIA is a meeting point for people interested in neural networks, deep learning and artificial intelligence. It is run mainly by Computer Science, Mathematics and Systems Engineering students at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, Medellín campus. We take a theoretical and practical approach. Every week we meet to learn about deep learning.

[cronograma.intro]

We will focus on the models known as LLMs (Large Language Models), modern language models built on the deep learning paradigm. There are no prerequisites to attend, and you don't need to have come last semester. [Click here for this semester's textbook](https://web.stanford.edu/~jurafsky/slp3/).

# ── Presentaciones ────────────────────────────────────────────────

[titulo.presentaciones]
talks

[presentacion.abrir]
Open talk →

[presentacion.intro.titulo]
Introduction to the group

[presentacion.intro.desc]
What AtarraIA is: the paradigm shift of neural networks, their applications, our sessions and events. Animated with manim; use the arrow keys to advance. In Spanish.

[presentacion.convolucion.titulo]
Convolution

[presentacion.convolucion.desc]
How a kernel slides over an image: the dot product in a window, the feature map, and the Sobel-X and Sobel-Y filters applied to a real photo. Animated with manim; use the arrow keys to advance. In Spanish.

[presentacion.lenguaje.titulo]
We need to talk

[presentacion.lenguaje.desc]
Language and LLM seminar: from ELIZA to agents, with an animation for each idea. Use the bar at the bottom or the arrow keys to advance. In Spanish.

[presentacion.tokenizacion.titulo]
Tokenization

[presentacion.tokenizacion.desc]
How text is split into tokens: morphemes, characters, ASCII, Unicode, UTF-8 and the BPE algorithm, and why an LLM struggles to count the r's in strawberry. In Spanish.

[presentacion.entropia.titulo]
Information, Entropy & Language

[presentacion.entropia.desc]
From the Voyager Golden Record and the Arecibo message to Zipf's law and Shannon's entropy orders: how to tell whether a pulsar, a radio burst or a whale song has the structure of a language. In Spanish.

[presentacion.ngramas.titulo]
N-grams

[presentacion.ngramas.desc]
N-gram language models: language as a stochastic process, the Markov assumption, estimating probabilities by counting, perplexity and smoothing, with a bigram playground and a lab for generating text. In Spanish.

[presentacion.coocurrencias.titulo]
Embeddings

[presentacion.coocurrencias.desc]
Embeddings by counting neighbors: the vocabulary of a real corpus (UNAL chats, Wikipedia, subtitles, Reddit and Colombian literature), the co-occurrence matrix column by column, the cosine similarity between two words, and the columns projected with PCA and UMAP. In Spanish.
